# TP Operating Doctrine

## Mental Model Reset: What a TP Really Is in 2026

### Old Model (Dead)
- "We train people"
- "We deliver hours"
- "We submit attendance"
- "Government pays for training"

### New Model (Required)

You are a **Compliance + Outcome Delivery Organization**.

> The Government is **outsourcing risk** to you.
> If learners don't get certified *and* don't show income proof, you don't get paid.

### Role Reframe

| Role | What it actually means |
|------|----------------------|
| Training Provider | Project implementer for state livelihood goals |
| Trainer | Certified resource whose validity is auditable |
| Batch | A funded project with financial milestones |
| Student | A future data point for "livelihood achieved" |
| Assessment | A financial gate, not an academic test |

---

## One-Page Operating Doctrine

- **Mindset:** You are an incentive manager, not an educator
- **Core asset:** Compliance + documentation discipline
- **Revenue sensitivity:** Assessment pass rates
- **Survival lever:** District Skill Committees
- **Scale lever:** Hub-and-spoke + SHG partnerships

## Strategic Positioning

Given the Women's Micro-Entrepreneurship & Digital Growth QP, you are best positioned as:

> "A TP specializing in **post-skill income amplification**, not beginner skilling."

Attractive to:
- State Skill Missions
- CSR partners
- NULM / DDU-GKY pilots
- Lakhpati Didi–linked projects

---

## How to Talk to Government

### Never ask for "funding." Ask for:
- **Targets**
- **District quotas**
- **Women-focused outcomes**

Government officers think in **numbers**, not pedagogy.

### Speak "Viksit Bharat", Not "Empowerment"

**Bad pitch:** "We will train women in entrepreneurship."

**Good pitch:** "We will convert 300 SHG women earning ₹2,000/month into verified micro-entrepreneurs earning ₹10,000/month within 6 months, aligned to the Lakhpati Didi mission."

### Hub-and-Spoke

If you lack infrastructure:
- Partner with ITIs, Govt schools, colleges
- Evening / weekend batches
- You provide trainers + compliance
- Govt provides space + legitimacy

Many districts **prefer** this model.

## Scheme Selection

| Scheme | Use it when |
|--------|------------|
| PMKVY 4.0 | Quick STT, general women skilling |
| DDU-GKY | Rural poor, placement-linked |
| NULM | Urban women, self-employment |
| State Skill Missions | Faster sanctions, contextual flexibility |

**Most TPs survive on SSDMs**, not central schemes.

## The Three Questions (Your Ops Summary)

1. Can I **open** a batch safely?
2. Can I **defend** every rupee claimed?
3. Can I **prove** income outcomes?

Everything else is noise.
