Strategy Stages (Owned Here)

Stage 1

Ideate

Problem selection + outcome hypothesis. What changes in the learner's life after this course?

"If I cannot articulate a post-course state, stop."
Stage 2

Qualify

NSQF level reasoning, NOS alignment, scope boundaries. The ruler, not the permission form.

"What decisions will the learner make independently?"
Stage 3

Design

Curriculum architecture (not content), assessment philosophy, evidence plan, trainer fit.

"What must be practiced vs explained? What evidence can be produced during learning?"
