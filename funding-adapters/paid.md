# Paid Training Adapter — Strategic View

## How Paid Differs

- **You control everything** — pricing, quality bar, customer promise
- No external compliance layer
- Revenue = direct from learner (or employer)
- Failure cost = refund + reputation damage
- Growth engine = retention, referrals, repeat purchase

## Pricing Considerations

- Fee must reflect perceived value, not just cost
- Consider: base fee + completion bonus model
- Scholarship slots (funded by margin from full-fee learners)
- Corporate bulk pricing if employer-sponsored

## What Learners Care About

1. **Will I earn more after this?** (ROI)
2. **Is the certificate credible?** (brand value)
3. **Is the format convenient?** (time commitment)
4. **Can I see proof from previous learners?** (social proof)

## Evidence Strategy

- Completion certificates (your brand)
- Learner testimonials + case studies
- Before/after portfolio (WhatsApp catalog, pricing sheet)
- NPS / satisfaction surveys
- Referral tracking

## Cash Flow Model

- Upfront payment (full or installments)
- Risk: refunds, drop-offs, chargebacks
- Mitigation: clear refund policy, value front-loading
