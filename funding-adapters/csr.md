# CSR Funding Adapter — Strategic View

## How CSR Differs

- Sponsor expects **impact narrative**, not portal compliance
- KPIs negotiated, not mandated
- Reporting is periodic (quarterly/half-yearly), not real-time
- Evidence is sampled, not exhaustive
- Safeguarding and brand alignment matter

## What CSR Sponsors Care About

1. **Numbers:** How many women trained? How many completed?
2. **Stories:** 3–5 compelling success stories with photos
3. **Proof of change:** Before/after income comparison, formalization rate
4. **Brand visibility:** Photos of events, branding, social media mentions
5. **Sustainability:** What happens after the grant ends?

## Pitch Language

> "We will convert 300 SHG women earning ₹2,000/month into verified micro-entrepreneurs earning ₹10,000/month within 6 months."

Speak outcomes, not pedagogy.

## Cash Flow Model

- Usually milestone-based: enrollment → mid-term → completion → impact report
- Some sponsors do upfront + completion
- Always negotiate milestone definitions before signing

## Risk

Primary risk: **reputation**, not payment. A failed CSR program damages your brand with all future sponsors.
