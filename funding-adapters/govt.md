# Government Funding Adapter — Strategic View

## The Tranche Trap

Funding ≠ grant. It's **conditional reimbursement**.

| Stage | Risk if you fail |
|-------|-----------------|
| Tranche 1 (Enrollment) | Cash flow delay |
| Tranche 2 (Assessment) | 70% funding at risk |
| Tranche 3 (Livelihood proof) | Project loss |

You must **self-finance upfront** and recover later.

## Skill India Digital (SID) — Your Control Plane

SID is **not a registry**. It is the **payment engine**.

You need:
- TP registration approved
- TC (Training Center) geo-tagged and verified
- Trainers mapped to job roles
- Batches created correctly (any error blocks payment)

> **Golden rule:** *If it's not on SID, it didn't happen.*

## Infrastructure Is About Auditability, Not Comfort

| Requirement | Why it exists |
|------------|--------------|
| Skill India branding | Photo evidence for audit |
| Aadhaar biometric attendance | Prevent ghost classes & ghost learners |
| Trainers with SSC ToT | Legal defensibility of certification |
| Equipment mapped to job role | Assessment alignment |
| Internet uptime | Portal + biometric dependency |

## Organization-Level Readiness

✅ Legal Entity (NGO / Trust / Society / Pvt Ltd, 3+ years preferred)
✅ Audited financials (last 3 years)
✅ Board resolution authorizing skilling activity
✅ PAN, GST (if applicable), bank accounts in org name

## Scheme Selection

| Scheme | Use when |
|--------|---------|
| PMKVY 4.0 | Quick STT, general women skilling |
| DDU-GKY | Rural poor, placement-linked |
| NULM | Urban women, self-employment |
| State Skill Missions | Faster sanctions, contextual flexibility |

**Most TPs survive on SSDMs**, not central schemes.
