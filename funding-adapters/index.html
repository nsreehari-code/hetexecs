Funding Strategy

One engine, three adapters. What changes across Govt / CSR / Paid — and why. Operational checklists live in hetops.

The Core Insight

Government funding did not create complexity. Reality did. Even without government, the same lifecycle exists — only with lighter compliance. What changes is strictness, not structure.

The Four Switches

Switch A: Proof Standard

Govt Audited proof for payments (portal-valid, third-party assessment, livelihood proof)

CSR Impact proof for reporting (credible, sampled, evidence-backed)

Paid Market proof (completion + satisfaction + repeat + referrals)

Switch B: Compliance Load

Govt SID, biometric, ToT/SSC, standard templates

CSR MOUs, safeguarding, donor reporting, basic verification

Paid Consumer policy, privacy, receipts, brand quality

Switch C: Governance & Approvals

Govt RFP/Scheme rules; district/state approvals

CSR Sponsor expectations + outcome KPIs + audits

Paid Internal quality bar + customer promise

Switch D: Cashflow Model

Govt Tranche-based reimbursement

CSR Milestone-based grants or outcomes-based

Paid Upfront payments; refunds/chargebacks risk
