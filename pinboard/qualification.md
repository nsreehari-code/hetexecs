## Qualification Logic

- **NSQF L4 for WMDG:** Learner makes independent decisions about pricing, customer communication, digital presence. Not supervised work — autonomous micro-enterprise operation.
- **NSQF is a ruler, not a permission form.** Use it to sharpen scope even when not filing for recognition.
- **Key question:** "What decisions will the learner make independently?" — this separates L3 (helper) from L4 (operator) from L5 (supervisor).
- **Targeting L3 first for NCVET.** L4 deferred — assessment infrastructure not ready. Risk of failed certifications would damage credibility more than delayed L4 launch.
