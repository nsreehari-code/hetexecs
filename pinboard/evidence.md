## Evidence Patterns

### What constitutes proof (by funding model)
- **Govt:** Udyam certificate, live WhatsApp catalog, income proxy (bank statement, UPI transaction log), geo-tagged photos
- **CSR:** Impact narrative, sampled evidence, success stories, before/after comparison
- **Paid:** Completion certificate, customer satisfaction, repeat purchase, referral rate

### Income proxies that work
- UPI transaction screenshots (even ₹500 counts)
- WhatsApp order threads
- Market stall photos with price tags
- Udyam registration confirmation
