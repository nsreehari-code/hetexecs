## Ops & Compliance Learnings

*This lane accumulates hard-won operational knowledge across batches.*

### Known risks (from design phase)
- Biometric failure → must have backup devices + daily checks
- Aadhaar mismatch → fix before batch starts, not after
- Trainer ToT expiry → renew proactively, not reactively
- Assessment agency delay → buffer batch timelines by 2 weeks minimum
- Livelihood proof ambiguity → over-document, don't under-document

### Principle
> Trainers should **never** be sole custodians of compliance. Documentation Officer is a role, not a task.
