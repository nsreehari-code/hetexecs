Stage 1: Ideate

Problem selection + outcome hypothesis. Before skills, before curriculum — what changes in the learner's life?

Core Question

"If I cannot articulate a post-course state, stop."

Lens 1: Livelihood Truth (Before Skills)

Ask first: What changes after this course, in the learner's life?

  • Increased monthly income?
  • Formalized business?
  • New customer channels?
  • Reduced dependence?

This lens is common to all funding models — Govt (mandatory), CSR (impact), Paid (value proposition).

What You Create at This Stage

  • CCF Sections A–B (Identity + Outcome Hypothesis)
  • One-page "Outcome Promise"

Go / No-Go

If you cannot articulate 3 concrete post-course changes in the learner's observable life, this idea is not ready. Return to listening.
