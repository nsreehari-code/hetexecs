Stage 2: Qualify

NSQF level reasoning, NOS alignment, scope boundaries. Use the qualification framework as a design grammar, not a permission form.

Core Question

"What decisions will the learner make independently after this course?"

Why Qualify Even Without Govt Funding?

You do NOT "apply NSQF" because government asks. You use NSQF because it is a design grammar for outcomes.

Even if you never register the QP, never seek recognition — this thinking:

  • Sharpens scope
  • Prevents under/over teaching
  • Enables later alignment if needed

Think of NSQF as: A ruler, not a permission form.

What You Decide

  • Is this beginner / operator / specialist? (NSQF level)
  • What decisions will the learner make independently?
  • What responsibility level do they carry after?
  • Draft 3–6 competency units
  • Which existing NOS/QP does this resemble? (mapping note)

What You Create

  • CCF Section C (QF / NOS Alignment)
  • Simple "NOS-like" competency table (internal)
