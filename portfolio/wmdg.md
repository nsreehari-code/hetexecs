# Women's Micro-Entrepreneurship & Digital Growth (WMDG)

## Identity
- **Target Learner:** Women already earning small income (stitching, food, handicrafts)
- **Duration:** 8 weeks, hybrid, with live lab
- **NSQF Level:** 4 (independent decisions + digital operations + basic compliance)
- **Delivery:** In-person + WhatsApp + live lab

## Outcome Hypothesis
If we equip existing women micro-entrepreneurs with WhatsApp commerce, pricing discipline, and AI-assisted marketing:
- Monthly orders increase by 2–3x
- Business gets formalized (Udyam, digital storefront)
- Customer acquisition moves from word-of-mouth to digital channels

## Qualification Logic (CCF Section C)
**NSQF Level 4 reasoning:** The learner makes independent decisions about pricing, customer communication, and digital presence. This is not supervised work — it's autonomous micro-enterprise operation.

### Draft Competency Units
1. Product readiness & differentiation
2. Unit economics & pricing
3. Digital storefront setup (WhatsApp Business, Maps, UPI)
4. Customer communication workflows
5. Responsible AI use for content creation

### NOS Mapping Note
Resembles QP: **MEP/Q0102 — Micro Enterprise Development** under MEPSC.
Can align to PMKVY if SSC approves custom QP variant.

## Blueprint Versions
- **WMDG-v1** — 8 weeks, 3 sessions/week, in-person + WhatsApp (current)
- **WMDG-v2** — 6 weeks intensive, daily sessions, urban (planned)

## Strategic Notes
- Best positioned as "post-skill income amplification" — not beginner skilling
- Attractive to State Skill Missions, CSR partners, NULM/DDU-GKY pilots, Lakhpati Didi projects
- Assessment: practical workbook + live WhatsApp catalog demo + short viva + AI-generated social plan
